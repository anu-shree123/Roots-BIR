Welcome

Roots Bir is a small, calm home in a village near Bir Billing. Wake up to the Dhauladhar range, spend slow afternoons in the garden, and end the day with a bonfire or a film. Deer Park Institute is a ten-minute walk away.

Around Bir

Bir is known for paragliding, but most of its charm is in the slower things.

Paragliding
Tandem flights from Billing, about 14 km up the hill.
Monasteries
Deer Park Institute, Chokling Gompa and the Tibetan Colony.
Tea gardens
